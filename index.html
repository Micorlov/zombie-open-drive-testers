Help test Zombie Open Drive

An open-world zombie driving game for Android. Drive a sunset Miami strip, shoot the crowds from your car and upgrade your ride. Free, and I need 12 testers for 14 days.

How it works

  1. Enter the email of the Google account on your Android phone below.
  2. I add you to the test (usually within a day).
  3. Open the join link on that phone and tap Become a tester, then install the game from Google Play.
  4. Keep it installed for 14 days and play when you like. That is what counts for Google.
